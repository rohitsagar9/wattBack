Shading & tilt

Trees, poles, neighbouring modules and buildings clip the light — even partial shade on one cell drags the string down. Wrong tilt or orientation costs the same way, every single day.

High temperature

Panel voltage falls as cells heat — output drops ~0.4% per °C above 25 °C. Indian summers push cells past 65 °C: a clear, hot, “perfect” day is also the day physics takes its cut.

DC cable loss

Every metre of DC cable burns energy as heat — I²R losses that grow with current. Undersized or long runs quietly tax 1–3% before the inverter ever sees it.
