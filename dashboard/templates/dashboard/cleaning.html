How it works

diagnostic_model.log

The HSU soiling model accumulates dust from PM2.5/PM10 deposition and removes it with tiered rain wash: drizzle (<1 mm) does not clean (it smears), 1–10 mm gives a partial ~30% wash, >10 mm resets the panel. As of {{ cf.as_of }} on {{ site.name }}, panels sit at ratio {{ cf.soiling_ratio }} (1.0 = perfectly clean). Every day until the next wash, that ratio is a direct multiplier on expected generation — so cleaning captures expected × (1 − sr) per day.
